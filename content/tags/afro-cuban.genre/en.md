---
name: "Afro-Cuban"
definition: "Music from the Cuban son and rumba family and its descendants, such as son, mambo, salsa and cha-cha-chá, built on clave-based rhythm and a percussion ensemble. Not any Latin-flavoured pop or jazz that merely borrows a conga."
---
An umbrella for the Cuban son and rumba lineage: son, mambo, salsa, rumba and cha-cha-chá. The clave rhythms themselves are described in the rhythm facet, so they are not repeated here.
