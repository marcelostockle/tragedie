---
name: "Blues"
definition: "Music built on the blues form and its characteristic scales and phrasing. Not any music that merely sounds sad."
---
The blues has its own tonal language: a minor-pentatonic melody with a flattened fifth, sung or played over major or dominant-seventh chords. The "blue notes" are bent or lowered thirds, fifths and sevenths that sit between major and minor. The usual form is the twelve-bar blues (I–IV–V).

Rock and jazz both borrow this language. A track gets this tag when the blues is the framework, not just a pentatonic solo or a "bluesy" lick.
