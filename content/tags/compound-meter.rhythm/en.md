---
name: "Compound meter"
definition: "Beats that naturally divide into threes (6/8, 9/8, 12/8), giving a rolling, lilting pulse. Not waltz, and not swing or shuffle, which bend a duple subdivision."
---
Slow 12/8 blues and ballads land here or on `shuffle` depending on the feel: a steady, rolling triplet pulse is compound meter, a repeated driving long-short groove is shuffle.
