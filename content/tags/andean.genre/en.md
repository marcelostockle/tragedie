name: "Andean"
definition: "Music of the Andean highlands, carried by instruments like the quena, charango, zampoña and bombo, with the modal melodies and rhythms of that region. Not any folk or Latin music that merely uses a pan flute."

Latin baroque (colonial-era art music of the Andes and the missions) counts here as an influence, not a separate genre. Use this tag when the Andean sound is present, and add `classical` if the track is also art music.
