---
name: "Soundfont"
definition: "Instruments played from sample-based soundfont libraries: more realistic and polished than basic MIDI, but still audibly sampled or stiff. Not basic General MIDI, and not a sample library that passes for a live performance."
---
Applies only when it audibly sounds like this. Pair it with the tag of the instrument being imitated, such as `saxophone.winds`. Some artists still mix these libraries into their music.
