---
name: "Soundfont"
definition: "Instruments played from basic General MIDI sounds or sample-based soundfont libraries: thin, stiff and obviously synthetic or sampled imitations of real instruments, as in old game and computer music. Not a sample library that passes for a live performance."
---
Applies only when it audibly sounds like this. Pair it with the tag of the instrument being imitated, such as `saxophone.winds`. Some artists still mix these sounds into their music.
