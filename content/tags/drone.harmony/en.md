---
name: "Drone"
definition: "A single sustained note or fifth held beneath the music while the other parts move, including a held bass note under changing chords (a pedal point). Not a pad or a sustained chord."
---
Covers both a constant drone (as in bagpipes or sitar music) and a held bass note while chords change over it. If the bass note itself steps downward, use `descending-bass-line.harmony` instead.
